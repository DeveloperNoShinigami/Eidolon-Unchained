# Deity System

## Overview

Eidolon Unchained features a comprehensive deity system with 11 unique deities, each offering distinct progression paths, rewards, and AI-powered interactions. Players can choose to serve different deities, gain reputation, and unlock increasingly powerful abilities.

## Available Deities

### Elemental Deities

**Fire Deity - Pyros, Lord of Eternal Flame**
- **Theme**: Forge mastery, volcanic power, transformative fire
- **Progression**: Spark Touched → Flame Keeper → Fire Guardian → Inferno Master → Sovereign of Eternal Flame
- **Specialties**: Fire resistance, strength buffs, forge-related items
- **Chant**: Fires Communion (`eidolon:flame` + `eidolon:soul` + `eidolon:sacred`)

**Water Deity - Aquaria, Empress of Flowing Waters**
- **Theme**: Ocean depths, healing currents, life-giving waters
- **Progression**: Tide Touched → Current Rider → Wave Guardian → Ocean Master → Sovereign of All Waters
- **Specialties**: Water breathing, healing effects, ocean treasures
- **Chant**: Waters Communion (`eidolon:water` + `eidolon:soul` + `eidolon:sacred`)

**Earth Deity - Terra, Sovereign of Stone**
- **Theme**: Mountain strength, mineral wealth, geological power
- **Progression**: Stone Touched → Boulder Walker → Mountain Guardian → Earthen Master → Sovereign of All Stone
- **Specialties**: Mining bonuses, resistance effects, precious materials
- **Chant**: Earths Communion (`eidolon:earth` + `eidolon:soul` + `eidolon:sacred`)

**Air Deity - Zephyr, Master of Eternal Winds**
- **Theme**: Freedom of flight, atmospheric mastery, boundless skies
- **Progression**: Breeze Touched → Wind Rider → Storm Guardian → Sky Master → Sovereign of All Skies
- **Specialties**: Flight effects, speed boosts, atmospheric tools
- **Chant**: Airs Communion (`eidolon:air` + `eidolon:soul` + `eidolon:sacred`)

## Progression System

Each deity offers a 5-stage progression system based on reputation:

1. **Stage 1 (0 rep)**: Initial blessing and basic rewards
2. **Stage 2 (25 rep)**: Enhanced abilities and better items
3. **Stage 3 (50 rep)**: **Major milestone** with significant rewards
4. **Stage 4 (75 rep)**: **Major milestone** with powerful artifacts
5. **Stage 5 (100 rep)**: **Ultimate mastery** with legendary rewards

### Reputation Gain
- Complete deity-specific tasks and quests
- Perform actions aligned with the deity's domain
- Successfully use communion chants
- Honor the deity's principles in gameplay

## Patron System

Players can become patrons of specific deities through special ritual ceremonies:

### Patron Rituals
Each deity has a unique patron ritual performed at an Eidolon ritual brazier:

- **Required Items**: Specific reagents and pedestal items themed to the deity
- **Ritual Effects**: Visual and audio effects matching the deity's domain
- **Patron Benefits**: Enhanced AI interactions and exclusive prayer types
- **Codex Integration**: Full ritual instructions available in the Eidolon Codex

### Patron Benefits
- Access to advanced prayer types (blessing, guidance, mystery, etc.)
- Enhanced AI deity conversations with deeper personality
- Exclusive rewards and abilities
- Special recognition in deity interactions

## AI Integration

### Conversation System
All deities feature AI-powered conversations when using communion chants:

- **Context-Aware**: Considers player reputation, location, time, and actions
- **Dynamic Responses**: Adapts to player progression and deity relationship
- **Command Execution**: Can grant items, effects, and perform actions
- **Personality-Driven**: Each deity has unique speech patterns and behavior

### Prayer Types
Different prayer types offer varied interactions:

- **Conversation**: General dialogue and moderate rewards
- **Blessing**: Temporary effects and buffs
- **Guidance**: Wisdom and direction with potential rewards
- **Mystery**: Deep lore and secrets for advanced followers
- **Trial**: Challenges and tests (specific deities)
- **Growth**: Enhancement effects (nature-focused deities)

## Inter-Deity Relationships

Deities have complex relationships affecting patron interactions:

### Allied Deities
- **Twilight ↔ Light & Dark**: Balance requires both extremes
- **Fire ↔ Earth**: Forge and foundation partnership
- **Water ↔ Air**: Flow and movement synergy
- **Nature ↔ Overworld**: Life and growth alignment

### Neutral Relationships
Most deities maintain neutral stance, allowing multi-patron worship with careful balance.

### Exclusive Paths
Some progression paths may conflict - choose wisely based on desired playstyle.

## Getting Started

1. **Choose Your Deity**: Consider your playstyle and preferred rewards
2. **Craft Communion Chant**: Learn the required sign sequences
3. **Build an Effigy**: Required for all deity interactions
4. **Perform Initial Communion**: Begin building reputation
5. **Complete Patron Ritual**: Unlock advanced features (optional)
6. **Progress Through Stages**: Build reputation for better rewards

## Tips for Success

- **Consistency**: Regular deity interaction builds stronger relationships
- **Alignment**: Play in ways that honor your chosen deity's domain
- **Balance**: Consider multi-deity worship for diverse benefits
- **Exploration**: Each deity offers unique lore and world perspectives
- **Patience**: Higher-tier rewards require significant investment

## Technical Requirements

- **Effigy Setup**: Active effigy within range for all interactions
- **AI Configuration**: Valid AI provider and API key for conversations
- **Datapack Support**: All deities are datapack-configurable
- **Mod Dependencies**: Requires Eidolon: Repraised and Curios API
